Description: This assignment tasked me with writing code to control the illumination of LEDs in response to the pressing and holding of a button. I coded the choosing of which LED to illuminate each time to be random and for no LED to be chosen twice or more times in a row by the random assignment. Additionally, I coded the LED to remain illuminated as long as the button was pressed and only turn off after the button had been released.

Tips:
    - Continue to check code as you go, instead of writing an entire code and then checking it all at once. This makes it easier to identify and solve problems throughout the process and increases efficiency
    - Customize the names of "ints" and different variables so that they make sense to YOU. When a lot of variables are in play at once it can get confusing so making sure they are recognizable and you can discern between them is valuable and that's best done making conscious decisions about what they should be named.
    - Ask questions and use in-class work time productively. To overcome major hurdles and ask questions directly to the professor when they are present is exceptionally helpful and in this project specifically it helped me build a good foundation of code and ideas to work with as I continued.
