Final Project Description:
This final project is a 3-piece collection of textiles with a common theme that should showcase all of the skills and components we've learned and used throughout the Electronic Textiles course. For me, I have chose the theme of Frosty-the-Snowman, or more broadly Winter, and my three pieces are an eyemask, a plushy, and the plushy's corresponding blanket. Each piece has a unique function and showcases a unique skill from the class. 



Winter Eyemask Paper Prototype:
![Eyemask](https://leach2.github.io/assets/img/Eyemask.jpg)
Winter Eyemask Project Description:
The Winter Eyemask is the simplest of the three designs. A wearable textile measure to fit to the face and tightened until snug by a shoelace headband (not pictured), this eyemask will all the wearer to become a part of the winter wonderland with Frosty and his blanket. In addition to showcasing sewing skills and various techniques, a trio of battery-powered LEDs will be controlled by a simply switch to illuminate and give the mask extra wintry flair.

Frosty-the-Snowman Plushy Project Description:
The Frosty-the-Snowman Plushy is the most elaborate of the three pieces, and therefore also includes the most electronic components while showcasing the most coding, circuity, and sewing understanding/ability. The Plushy is in the shape of Frosty, with a round body, a round head, and a stubby pair of both arms and legs. His face consists of the classic button eyes, carrot nose, and coal-lump smile, all of which will be sewn. He also has two buttons on his body, the third of which will be the RBG LED set, the first of the electronic components. This component will illuminate blue when Frosty is "feeling" cold, and then red if not or if his blanket it attached. The other electronic components are on the back of Frosty's body, and attached to the LilyPad Arduino. A temperature sensor is the most important of the additions, and it will be set to read the temperature of Frosty's surroundings, triggering other actions depending on if Frosty is "feeling" cold or not. If he is feeling cold, the vibe board will vibrate to the tune of Frosty's theme song "Frosty-the-Snowman", in addition to the RDG LEDs turning blue as aforementioned.

Frosty-the-Snowman's Blanket Project Description:
The Blanket is the final piece, and is an interactive add-on to the Frosty-the-Snowman Plushy. Two snaps on each the blanket and Frosty's back allow for the blanket to be attached. When the blanket is not attached it is dormant, it is only when attached that the electronic components can function. When attached by the snaps, the blanket will illuminate both of its own LEDs a warm red color. Additionally, Frosty will react to the attachment of the blanket as well and his actions will change. Regardless of how cold the temperature sensor is reading, the Plushy will switch away from its cold reactions and the music will stop. Additionally, the RGB LED on Frosty's front will switch from Blue to Red to symbolize his feeling the warmth of the blanket.
