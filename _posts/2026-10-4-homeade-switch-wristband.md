Project Description:
The objective of this project was to build a wearable illuminating wristband using a battery holder, one LED, a pair of snaps, conductive thread, colored (green) thread, colored (blue) felt, and another conductive material for an additional switch of your choice. The goal was to properly assemble all of the pieces so that the LED would indeed illuminate while also demonstrating an understanding of switches so that your own switch could be seamlessly integrated into the rest of the design. I chose a swinging paper clip as my other switch and overlayed a striped thread design for additional aesthetic. I was successful in the construction of this project because my wristband fits my wrist and is wearable, and the LED also illuminates when the circuit is closed.

Tip/Trick/Hint for Others:
I struggled with aligning the snaps properly so that the wristband would be wearable once fully constructed, wasting me valuable time and also materials. My tip for others or for me in the future attempting similar projects would be to be especially deliberate and ironclad in the understanding of switch connections. Switches are tricky, especially ones with a wrap-around nature such as those on this wristband, and for this reason it is worth taking an extra second to ensure that everything is properly aligned before permanently attaching anything. I would recommend going as far as to lay all materials on the felt before beginning construction just so there is a clear visual of the plan for building.
