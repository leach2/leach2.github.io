Hello friendly visitors, my name is Ryan and I am a first-year at Kenyon College in Gambier, OH. I am from Chatham, NJ and am 18 years old. I have a passion for music, reading, and being outside. However my greatest passion is for basketball and I am on the team here at school. 

I am interested in Electronic Textiles because of the variety of skills I hope to learn and their potential application to my everyday life. In a world that's becoming more and more digitized everyday, the value of being able to code has never been greater and I'm really excited to learn about the basics of that during this course. I also think sewing and hands-on skills are the kind that you never lose in life and never know when you might need.

I am also undecided on my major and Electronic Textiles is especially interesting to me for that reason too. This class represents a step out of may comfort zone and I hope that it alongside the other courses I'm taking in a variety of different departments will help point me towards a path to a potential major.
